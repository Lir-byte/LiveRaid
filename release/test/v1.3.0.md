# LiveRaid 1.3.0-test

Test build after the analysis of 8 raids of 1.2.9 on Customs (2026-11-09).

## Learned shortcuts are on everywhere
- F12 "Use learned shortcuts" is on by default, in every preset (Fixes only included); an existing config is switched once (marker "Learned shortcuts revision").
- Doubtful climbs are dropped when loaded (shorter than 3 m, longer than 45 m, a rise above 9 m, broken numbers); the log says how many were taken. A climb you do not want: "Enabled": false in learned/<map>.shortcuts.json.
- A stop at one place longer than 90 s counts as 90 s (the old file had one of 2424 s).

## Packrat
- Only a lone PMC, as before - and now a tagged kit that landed in a squad stays an ordinary member of it; nobody is pulled out of a group any more.
- Server: three candidate kits per side instead of one (the 16:56 raid tagged two and neither was ever in the client pool).
- "Packrat is a vacuum": nearest and richest quests first, no "loot only on the way" drive, a flight from a fight is a slip 70-110 m away; second flight in 3 minutes or under 60 % health - to an exit. Its weapon is not stronger.

## The map remembers cold places
- learned/<map>.atlas.json (the first one is built from your coverage CSVs: tools/atlas_bootstrap.py); report atlas/<map>.csv with a reason for every spot nobody reached.
- Spots missed in 2+ raids in a row become "LR Explore S<k>" quests (up to 24 clusters of 14, floor by floor) and weigh more.

## Keys and doors
- A key rolled for a follower goes to the squad leader (85 %), into its secure container (invisible, not lootable; no container or room - it stays with the follower); a door near the bot weighs more (x1.8 within 60 m); a keyed door step is dropped after 5.5 minutes without getting closer (was 2.5); errand chance 85 %.

## Squads and floors
- Followers look into places 12-30 m from the leader (a floor up included) for up to 40 s.
- Pull of the upper floors: gain x8, up to x3 (was x3 and x2.2).

## To check in a raid
`LR Atlas:` lines, `LR Trail: N of M learned shortcuts ... taken as routes`, `LR Key: the key ... goes to its squad leader`, `squad members sent to look into nearby places`, `packrat vacuum:`, atlas/bigmap.csv after a raid longer than 8 minutes.
