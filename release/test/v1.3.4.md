# LiveRaid 1.3.4-test

After the raids of 1.3.3 on 2026-11-10: Customs 11:43 (40 min), Reserve 12:29 (3 min) and Reserve 12:37 (28 min).

## Fixed
- **A bot told to leave on a map with no exit open to bots became a bystander** (rasty_airsoft, Reserve, from minute 8 to the end of the raid: 19 minutes round one building in a 30 m square, pressing into walls and doors). The game's bot exfil only goes to an exit with no requirement, and Reserve has none for a PMC (D-2 and the bunker door need a switch, the manhole an empty backpack slot, the cliff a paracord and an ice axe, the train is a train). The order to leave was given all the same: QuestingBots stopped the bot's questing for good and LiveRaid's own systems skipped it as "leaving", while the exfil layer never came on (0 samples in the whole raid; three bots were sent out and none left). Now (built in, always on - no F12 switch): before the order is given - LiveRaid's own or QuestingBots' end-of-raid one - a PMC with no usable exit takes the switch exit whatever the odds (pulls the switches, stands the timer); with none of those either, or for a player scav, the order is refused and the bot goes on with its raid and is asked again when an exit opens. A bot that already wants to leave and has lost its way out (the switch plan failed, the car left) gets its raid back: questing and looting again.
- **A bot on a navmesh island stood for minutes** (Юра Вольфенштейн, Customs, Warehouse 17: 6 minutes on the spot). The check for a way out ran only after three more failed paths - and a bot with no quest step left makes none; it looked 15 and 30 m around on its own level only, and the later "wedged" rescue moved him 6 m along the same island. Now the check repeats by itself every 15 s (up to 8 times, also when the camera was on the bot the first time), looks 6-45 m around and up to 9 m up or down, takes the nearest point from which a full path leads to the open map, and the "wedged" rescue of a bot on an island does the same instead of a 3-8 m step.
- **The packrat went home in the first minutes with next to nothing** (Jiggle_Peek, Customs: sent out at 5:10 after two meetings with enemies, 14 things looted, 4 minutes on the way to the paid car, shot there at 11:51). The second flight within 3 minutes now sends it to an exit only when there is something to carry home (150k+ or a full bag), after 10 minutes in the raid, or after the fourth flight; otherwise it slips 130-170 m away (70-110 m the first time) and goes on looting there. Hurt under 60 % it leaves as before.
- **Error in "shuts the door behind it"** on a door with no handle on record (EFT's `GetClosestGrip` threw, one error on Reserve): such a door stays open.

## What to look for
- `LR Extract: ... leave through switch exit ... - no other exit of this map is open to bots`, `... would leave, but no exit of this map is open to it ... stays in the raid`, `... was on its way out, but ... - it goes on with its raid`; in `LR Health on`: `no exit open to bots: N sent through a switch exit instead, N stayed in the raid, N given their raid back`.
- On Reserve: no PMC in one building for more than a few minutes after an `LR Extract:` line about it; whether anyone pulls the D-2 switch and leaves (`left the raid through switch exit`).
- `LR Stuck: ... is shut in ... -> put back outside` within a minute of the first `is shut in` line; no `standing still` run over 2 minutes in `tools/stalls.py`.
- `LR Packrat: ... slips away ... - the second time in 3 minutes, but with ~Nk after N min it is too early to go home`; how long the packrat lives and how many loot jobs it finishes.
