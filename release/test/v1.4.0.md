# LiveRaid 1.4.0-test

The whole map: bunkers, basements, the corners between the scav yards - and a raid that does not empty out. On top of 1.3.4-test (not played yet), after the raids of 2026-11-10 (Customs 40 min, Reserve 28 min).

What the logs showed: on Reserve all the bots together finished 93 quest steps in 28 minutes against 714 spots under watch (59 % visited), on Customs 470 in 40 minutes against 767 (73 %). Of the 286 spots nobody reached on Reserve, 115 were passed by on another floor - 77 of them lay 7-17 m under the ground (the bunkers). Seventeen player scavs in a row came in "to the fight" at the same two squares and died there, so the people on the map stayed at 3-5 of 8 wanted all raid.

Every new rule is a weight or a chance rolled per bot, each with its switch in F12.

## The whole map
- **Skip places just checked** (F12 3, with a strength %). QuestingBots hands a bot the nearest step of its quest whether or not somebody stood there a minute ago. A step whose spot any bot has visited in the last 10 minutes is now refused by chance (open ground 90 %, a loot spot 55 % - passing by is not searching; less for an old visit, more for explorers; a bot's roll for a spot holds 3 minutes) - but only while the same quest still has ground nobody has seen, so a quest never runs out of steps because of it. If QuestingBots finds no quest at all for a bot, the rule is lifted for that bot for 10 minutes instead of its questing being switched off.
- **Pull of the depths** (F12 3, with a strength %). The ground level is taken per 50 m square (the median of the open-ground spots round it); a spot 3.5 m or more under it is "deep". A quest with a third or more of its spots deep weighs up to x2.3 while they are fresh, to each bot's own appetite (explorers, looters and rats go down, snipers and campers seldom). "The depths" is also a new aim of the raid (explorers 2.0, looters and rats 1.2, snipers 0.2).
- **Wandering scav groups** (F12 3, 25 %). A share of the AI scav groups has no patrol area and walks the whole map, as some scavs do online; the rest keep to their yards as before.
- **Replacements come in where nobody has been** (F12 6 "Replacements to the fight, %", 35). After a loud fight a brought-forward player scav group goes to the fresh bodies only with this chance; the rest come in at the region with the most unvisited ground, away from the last 5 minutes' deaths, never within 150 m of a human or 60 m of a living bot.

## Leaving
- **Leave the backpack for an exit** (F12 8, chance 60 %). A "no backpack" exit (the sewer manhole of Reserve) is now an exit for a bot that would drop its bag: rolled once per bot (a bag under 80k - as set, 80-250k - half, more - a fifth, the greedy and the packrat next to never). It walks there like to any exit and throws the backpack off 25 m short of it. With 1.3.4's rule Reserve now has two ways out for bots: the manhole and the D-2 switch.

## People
- **Packrat from the start** (F12 6). Its group is put first among the PMC groups still to come, instead of arriving with the reserve at minute 5.
- **Remembers where people died** (F12 4, with a strength %). Deaths of people in the last 8 minutes that a bot knows of (a squadmate's counts double; others within 250 m) make that part of the map hot for it. A roll per bot by character: the wary (rats, campers, looters) keep away - quests there weigh down to x0.35; hunters and rushers are drawn - up to x1.6; the rest hardly care; a hurt bot is never drawn.

## Not done
- The armoured train as a bot exit (no navmesh obstacle for the train; SAIN leaves it out for the same reason).
- Hearing by surface and floor: NoiseResponse already halves the range through a wall and cuts it to 40 % across a floor - nothing new was added.
- A personal pace and route habits: Tempo and the squad doctrines already give that.

## What to look for
- `LR Coverage: N of M spots visited` every 5 minutes against 31/43/51/58/59 % (Reserve) and 32/45/53/60/65/68/71/73 % (Customs).
- In `LR Health on`: `skip places just checked: N steps ... refused`, `depths: N spots under the ground, N quests go down`, `places of death remembered: ...`, `no-backpack exits: ...`, `scav patrols: N areas (N wander the map ...)`.
- `LR Depths: N of M spots lie 3.5 m or more under the ground`, `takes an aim: the depths`, bots at heights -14..-18 on Reserve in the timeline.
- `LR PScav: ... to ground nobody has been to round (x, z)` and how long those scavs live; `LR Population: ... people N of M wanted`.
- `LR Extract: ... throws off its backpack ... at the exit`, `LR Packrat: the packrat's group was no. N ... it comes in first`.
- Must not appear: `LR Coverage: ... found no quest with the places just checked left out` more than a few times a raid; QuestingBots' `cannot select any quests`.
