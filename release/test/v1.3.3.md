# LiveRaid 1.3.3-test

After the 25-minute Customs raid of 1.3.2 (2026-11-09 22:18).

## Fixed
- **Endless reload** (42MadBeast: 53 swaps in 3 minutes, no shot fired, an old bug). EFT swaps in the *fullest* magazine within reach without checking that it holds more than the one in the gun, and SAIN reloads whenever the gun's magazine is under 80 %. With every spare magazine at 15 of 20 the bot swapped one 15 for another every 3-4 s and SAIN's "Reload" self-action never ended. A swap that would add no rounds is now refused (in `CanReload`, so SAIN does not start it, and again in `ReloadMagazine`), and a loaded gun with three swaps in 14 s is left alone for 20 s (built in, always on - no F12 switch).
- **Spawns by zone** (F12 "Rotate the starting zones"): inside its side of the map a squad took a random point, so zones with 9 points came up three times as often as zones with 3. Now each 90 m zone remembers its starts (`learned/<map>.spawns.json`, faded 15 % a raid) and weighs `1/(1+starts)^1.2`, zones equal whatever the number of their points; zones near the atlas's cold corners and near the locked room of an EFT quest (the Customs terminal) get up to twice the weight.
- **Keys to whoever starts near the door** (built in, always on - no F12 switch): the key of the Customs terminal ("A package from the past", container 6.0 upstairs behind a locked door) was never carried from the start in 6 raids. A key now weighs x3 for a bot starting near its door, x2.5 when no one has it yet this raid, x2.5 for a door that holds an EFT quest item; such a door pulls harder in the own key errand wherever the bot is.

## Checked, nothing to fix
- The packrat comes (T0mmyMushrooms, a lone PMC, Looter, no weapon of its own beyond a sawn-off), slips away from fights (11 times), left early at minute 9 and was killed 6 minutes later on the way to ZB-1011 with both legs blacked. It did not cut the lobby: planned 12, alive at 2 minutes 12.

- **The camera did not follow the packrat**: the observer-raid camera was armed only when "Follow a random PMC at the start" was on, so with that off (as in the user's config) and "Follow the packrat at the start" on, nothing was ever followed. The packrat switch now works on its own: the camera waits for the packrat (up to 3 minutes) and stays free if it does not come; with the random-PMC switch also on, the fallback to a random PMC is as before.
