# LiveRaid 1.3.1-test

After the first raid of 1.3.0 (Customs, 30 min, 2026-11-09 20:16).

## Fixed
- **Arena fighters (Hardbass, Mikhalych, Kulyok, Lobster...) never shot back** - 18 hits taken, 0 shots at PMCs; they stood in a patrol layer and aimed without a shot. SAIN removes their vanilla fight layers and gives them none of its own; the vanilla ones are put back before each raid (F12 "Arena fighters fight back").
- **Packrat did not come with the chance at 100 %**: the server tags the kit in the very request of the FIRST group, which is usually a squad's. Now the first PMC group of an armed raid is made a group of one (a lone PMC from the start, nobody is pulled from a group).
- **Looting through a locked door** (military checkpoint): a locked door between the bot and the thing now counts as a wall (LootReach).
- **The collar**: the leader no longer stops and turns the second a follower lags (debounce 3.5 s) and the ranges are wider (6-22 m moving, 10-35 m in a fight, wait past 24 m, regroup past 42 m).
- Learned shortcuts: 7 of the 13 Customs climbs lay in one 8 x 18 m pocket; one per pocket now, none that start and end within 4 m.

## New
- Squads: the formation breathes (wider on calm ground, closes up in danger; "heap" squads stay tight), roles change on the way (drift by weights, new roles flanker and scout), new formations (column, echelon, diamond, spread), 4 new doctrines (Scouts, Ambushers, Wolves, Support), 13 new quirks.
- Help: a badly hurt squadmate with an enemy in view calls for help, the call repeats after 7 s (was 12), a mate in a far fight helps when the victim is under 45 %.

## Measured in the 1.3.0 raid
Atlas sweep quests: 197 steps done (the ordinary explore quests: 195); the block of 131 spots at (87, -162) that nobody had reached in 13 raids: 130 reached.
