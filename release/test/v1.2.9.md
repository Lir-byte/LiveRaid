# LiveRaid 1.2.9 (test)

Made on top of 1.2.8 at the owner's request: the bots explore more actively, with aims of their own, without touching how they navigate. Everything here only changes the WEIGHT of the quests QuestingBots picks anyway (or the moment a layer flips); nobody is led by force, every feature has its F12 switch.

- **Aims of the raid** (F12 -> 3 "Aims of the raid", `Bots/RaidAgenda.cs`). Every PMC and player scav rolls once a raid (70 %, "share of bots") whether it plays by aims; the rest go as before. A directed bot keeps 2-4 aims: a REGION to loot (rich, near, not yet gone through by others), a QUEST of its own (QuestingBots' map quests and the game's, from the ones it may take), the HEIGHTS (quests with spots upstairs), a COLD corner (a region nobody has set foot in). Character decides the mix: looters aim at regions, questers at quests, explorers at heights and cold corners, snipers at heights. An aim makes the quests that serve it weigh 1.6-2.4x more (each bot to its own degree, 0.75-1.25), capped at 3.2x; reconsidered every 70-110 s - an aim others have since covered, whose time is up, or when the bot heads for the exit, is dropped and a new one drawn from the map as it looks now. Trace: `takes an aim: ...`, summary `aims of the raid: N bots directed ...`.
- **Upper floors** (F12 -> 3 "Pull of the upper floors", `Raid/VerticalLedger.cs`). The share of the bots' time spent upstairs (a point 2.5 m above the lowest spot of its 10 m column, as `tools/coverage.py` counts) is kept against the share of the map's loot spots that are upstairs (Customs: 12 % of the time against 28 % of the loot). While the bots stay down, quests with spots upstairs weigh up to x2 more, while they crowd upstairs, less (x0.75); each bot to its appetite for heights (sniper 1.5, explorer 1.4 ... camper 0.6). Summary `upper floors: ...`.
- **Cold corners** (F12 -> 3 "Pull of the cold corners"). A region quest whose spots nobody has visited pulls up to +45 % harder from minute 4 to minute 12 (on top of the fresh-ground pull of 1.2.6); the cold-corner aim sends explorers there at once.
- **A full bag is a reason to leave, not a rule** (F12 -> 2 "A full bag is a reason to leave"). The early-leave weighing counted a full bag only when half the squad had one. Now a bag that has stayed full adds up to 0.4 of urge, by character: the careful and rats leave sooner, a greedy bot whose bag holds cheap things stays to find better (the swap of cheap for dear is MakeRoom's, already there), one with a rich bag (150k+) has no more reason to stay.
- **A found key goes to the squad** (F12 -> 2 "Found key goes to the squad", `Bots/SquadKeys.cs`). A squadmate who finds a key to a door of this map tells the leader, and the key goes to him with the squad's own handover (walk up, throw, pick up) in a quiet moment, within 5 minutes: the leader is the one who picks the quest of the room. `LR Key: X tells Y of the key to ...`, summary `found keys told to the squad N, handed to the leader M`.
- **The squad remembers emptied buildings** (F12 -> 2 "The squad remembers emptied buildings"). A building a squad member has gone through to the end, and a room opened with a key and found empty, is not looted again by the rest of the squad for 10 minutes (until a body falls there). Summary `emptied buildings told to squadmates N`.
- **One arbiter of the layer flicker** (`LayerCalm`). The hold of the flips of QuestingBots' idle layer now also covers LootingBots' "Looting" layer (1.5 s; "Looting <-> PatrolAssault" came 100-140 times a raid on Customs). Never in a real fight or while treating - those answers go through at once; the Peaceful action runs in the gaps.
