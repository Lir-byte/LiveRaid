# LiveRaid 1.2.7 (test)

Made after the first raid on 1.2.6 (Ground Zero, 2026-11-07 18:57, 12.8 min, `errors none`).

- **What the 1.2.6 raid showed.** "Online-like spawns" works again (14 squads on random points, 7 starting squads at a nearest-neighbour distance of 60 m min / 94 m median, the reserve held back 29 times, player scavs placed at the fights); the coverage ledger runs (`LR Coverage`: 58 % of the spots by minute 4, 67 % by minute 9, 1 of 10 regions never entered); no QuestingBots/LootingBots errors from LiveRaid, no repeated key-move errors, layer ping-pongs 1.3 per bot-minute (as before). Not shown yet: that the walk of the map got better - the PMCs' own share of loot spots reached stood at 33 % in this short, fight-heavy raid (32-40 % in the earlier Ground Zero raids), more raids are needed.
- **A bot wedged in a fight is put back on the navmesh.** In that raid Bratanoff stood at one point to the centimetre for 3+ minutes (SeekCover / Search / Assist in turn, "EFT path False", an open door beside him, the log closed at 12:47 before the 200 s Rescue of 1.2.5 could act - and that one looks only at bots in the quest layer). `FrozenWatch` now counts the "wants to move and does not" notes per place: three of them within 2 minutes at the same place (the bot did not leave a 1.5 m circle for 120 s, whatever it was busy with) put it on the navmesh 3-8 m away (the farthest free spot), unseen by the camera (after 7 minutes even seen): `LR Stuck: ... wedged for ... -> put on the navmesh`, summary `put back on the navmesh after minutes in one place N`.
- **Kills in the trace now show the kind of damage** (`Pycak killed Pycak (LeftLeg, Bullet)`): "X killed X" came up once more (1 of 36) - whether it is a grenade, bleeding or a mis-attributed shot will be seen from the damage type.
- Version 1.2.7 in the client (both editions), the server and the launcher.
