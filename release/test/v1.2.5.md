# LiveRaid 1.2.5 (test)

- **Keys in the pouch.** A key a bot went into the raid with now lies, by chance, in its secure pouch instead of its pockets: it cannot be seen on the body or taken. Two new F12 settings in "Keys and doors": **"PMC key in the pouch, chance"** (85 % by default, so 15 % of PMC keys stay in the pockets, rig or backpack) and **"Scav key in the pouch, chance"** (50 % by default). A compact level "Keys in the pouch" sets both (in plain sight / rarely / like online / almost always). It covers the keys LiveRaid hands out and the keys SPT spawned the bot with (a scav's backpack loot): each is rolled on its own and moves into the pouch if it wins; no pouch or no room left the key where it was. Prize keys stay in the pouch as before. The raid summary gets a line `keys of the bots: N dealt into the pouch, M in the open, K that SPT spawned moved into the pouch`.
- **Guns of WTT-ContentBackport for the bots.** The mod registers the weapons of the 1.0 update, but its "addtoBots" only copies an item into the bots' *loot* - no bot ever carried one of its guns. Now the server (`data/config.json`, `bots.modWeapons`) puts the mod's 14 guns, 4 knives and 13 rounds into the equipment and ammo pools of the PMCs (usec, bear) and, when cheap enough (up to 120 000 roubles: the M16A1/A2), of the scavs, at the average weight of the pool. Each gun gets a mod pool built from the gun it was cloned from (cut down to what its slots accept) and its stock build; a gun that cannot be built is left out. Tested on the server: 900 generated PMCs, about 10 % carry a mod gun as the primary weapon (13 of the 14 seen), no warnings about missing parts. `modFolders`, `weightOfAverage`, `scavMaxPrice`, `knives`, `ammo`, `exclude` and `enabled` are in the config (restart the server).
- **After a take-over the map no longer freezes (Perfect Culling).** The level's occlusion system lives ON the camera; a take-over makes EFT rebuild the camera and the destroyed sampler's `Shutdown` disposed the map-wide grids and switched `ScreenDistanceSwitcher` off for good - houses, objects and details never switched again ("textures stopped loading", state line `occlusion: none`). While LiveRaid rebuilds the camera that shutdown is now skipped (`Spectator/CullingGuard.cs`, F12 "Keep level culling alive through a take-over") and the new camera gets a sampler if its prefab brought none.
- **Smoothie.** Its experimental "Hide objects covered by nearer ones" and "Skip LOD work for objects you cannot see / too far to draw" work out visibility for ONE camera; after the first take-over of a raid they are switched off in memory (the file is not written) until the raid ends and what they hid is shown again (`Spectator/SmoothieGuard.cs`, F12 "Pause Smoothie culling after a take-over"). The state line now also shows Smoothie's depth status.
- **A grenade in a taken-over bot's hands is thrown.** EFT's `BaseGrenadeHandsController.Throw` takes the throw from the AI brain's `AIGreanageThrowData` for an AI bot in the Active state (a taken bot stays Active) - nobody fills it for a body you play: an exception, caught inside, and a silent return after the animation. The throw of a taken bot now goes the way of your own character's (`TakenBotGrenadePatch`).
- **F12 "Content Backport guns for bots"** (Spawns): exists only when WTT Content Backport is installed (its client part in BepInEx/plugins); on/off sent to the server (`/liveraid/modweapons/set/0|1`) - off takes the guns out of the pools (tested: 0 % of 600 PMCs with a mod gun, on - 7.5 %).
- **One archive.** `LiveRaid-<v>.zip` (and `LiveRaid-Test-<v>.zip`) holds both editions: the single-player client works after unpacking, Online sits in `LiveRaid Launcher/editions/Online` and `LiveRaid Launcher.exe` switches to it. No more `LiveRaid-Online-<v>.zip`.
- **A bot wedged for minutes is put back on the navmesh.** Raid 2026-11-06 00:01: Nikita_Pls stood at one point to the centimetre for 8+ minutes after a container search (side steps, pushes and back-offs did nothing - the body did not even turn). After 200 s without moving, with a goal to walk to, the bot is put 3-8 m away on the navmesh toward the goal, out of sight (after 7 minutes even in view): `LR Stuck: ... wedged for ... -> put on the navmesh`.
- Quieter log: `LR Frozen ... cannot walk and no treatment is to blame` only after 5 s of it (423 notes in one raid were EFT's momentary "obstacle in front of the hands", the bots walked on).
- **PMC gun condition in F12** (Spawns): the values of `bots.pmcWeaponCondition` are now F12 settings you change live: "PMC gun condition like online" (on/off), "Brand-new gun chance, %" (15), "Repairs: max above current, points" (8) and four condition levels, each with "from, %", "to, %" and "weight" (80-95 % weight 22, 65-80 % 60, 50-65 % 14, 35-50 % 4; weight 0 = the level never comes up). They go to the server at the start of the game and on every change (`/liveraid/weaponcondition/set/...`, 15 numbers) and stand over the config, which keeps the start values. Tested on the server (150 PMCs each): defaults - average ~75 %, 17 % new; off - 93 %; only level 20-30 % - all guns 20-30 %; only level 80-95 % - 80-95 %.
- The Smoothie setting exists only when Smoothie is installed (a folder or DLL named Smoothie in BepInEx/plugins).
- The archive has two equal folders, `Single` and `Online`, each a complete package for the game's root (see INSTALL.md).
- **Content Backport clothes on the PMC bots.** WTT-ContentBackport registers its outfits but nothing put them into the bots' looks (checked: 0 pieces in the USEC/BEAR pools). Now the server (`bots.modClothes`, `Core/ModClothes.cs`) adds its 58 tops and 34 bottoms to the Body/Feet pools of the roles the outfit's `side` allows (USEC pieces to usec, BEAR to bear, shared to both), each with the weight SPT's own looks have (all 1): every look of a pool, the mod's and SPT's, is picked with the same chance - no chance setting. Each top gets its own hands (otherwise SPT takes random ones). One F12 switch (Spawns, only with Content Backport installed): "Content Backport clothes for bots". Tested on the server: off - 0 WTT looks; on - the WTT share equals the share of its pieces in the pools (about 68 % of tops and 63 % of bottoms, since the mod has twice as many).
- **AllTheClothes - WTT addon** (separate server mod, `Projects/AllTheClothesWTT`): opens the 92 outfits of Content Backport for Ragman (no requirements) and both factions as AllTheClothes does for the vanilla ones, and now also unlocks its 11 heads and 14 voices for every profile (`default` entries in the customisation storage) - no chat command needed.
- Version 1.2.5 in the client (both editions), the server and the launcher.
