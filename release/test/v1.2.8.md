# LiveRaid 1.2.8 (test)

Made after the raids on 1.2.7 (Ground Zero: 4 full ones and one abandoned at once; Customs: 2 full ones and 2 short ones; 2026-11-07 19:20 - 2026-11-08 02:04).

- **PMCs no longer go in without spare magazines (server).** Customs 00:09: h4rd111 fired the one magazine in his gun in the first fight, had 760 loose rounds in his bag and nothing to put them in, and finished the fight with a knife. SPT puts spare magazines only into the rig and pockets and fails quietly when they do not fit: of 300 generated PMCs 57 had no spare magazine and 42 had one. A new server patch (`bots.pmcSpareMagazines`, on) tops the starting kit up to 2-5 spare magazines of the main gun's own magazine, full of the round it is loaded with: rig, then pockets, then the bag / secure container (the client's MagKeeper carries them to the rig). After: 3 of 300 with none, 11 with one (shotguns and bolt guns). Nothing is made during the raid. `tools/pmc_loot_test.py` prints the spread.
- **A bot stuck in one place with no enemy about is put back on the navmesh, farther each time.** Customs 01:29: the scav Fedor Tridemaks stood 16 minutes in front of the dorms door (an open leaf pressed into him, "stuck at door ... opens it" 90 times); the old wedge watch only counted while a SAIN decision against a known enemy ran, the quest-layer rescue did not apply to the patrol layer, and the one rescue he got put him 7 m away in the same trap. Now any bot (not a boss / guard) that has not left a 1.5 m circle for 3 minutes and is not doing something that stands still by nature (loot job, treatment, reload, exit wait, a task with time left, a fight it can see) is rescued - toward its quest goal when it has one, 3-8 m first, 8-16 m and 16-32 m if it is wedged again at the same place within 15 minutes. `LR Stuck: ... rescue no. N at this place`, summary `of those with no enemy about N`.
- **A bot with a door key makes its door its own errand.** 18-25 bots a raid carried a door key, 1-6 of them ever held the quest of their door, 0-2 doors a raid were opened (Customs: 36 carriers, none opened; the locked-room quest lost to every other quest of QuestingBots). F12 -> 2 "Own key, own door": each key carrier rolls once per raid (65 %) whether this is its errand; then its door's quest weighs 3.5x. Summary `key errands: N of M`.
- **Spawn trace shows how near the squads started to you.** `start spread: ..., nearest squad start to you X m, squads within 150 m / 300 m of you A / B (only 25 m round your point is kept free)`. The player's spawn does not forbid anyone: only the 25 m round the point is kept free and QuestingBots' 15 m minimum applies; in the last Customs raid the nearest squad of 12 simply started 153 m away.
- **A scav with a full bag no longer walks to and fro to "look" at the same body.** Ground Zero 23:32, top floor of Capital Insight: a scav with a full bag "went to look" at one ~121k body 29 m away every minute and QuestingBots' quest steps took the brain back after seconds each time (it also walked the five-objective "Capital Insight" tour of nine steps in 25 m, "completed a step after 8-18 s"). MakeRoom's scouting now makes two tries per body, then leaves it for 5 minutes.
- **Packrat trace says why.** `packrat pool: ... PMC groups planned N, the tagged profile was not in the pool M times` (an armed raid on 2026-11-05 gave no packrat and the trace did not say why). Over 20 raids since 1.2.4 the packrat was armed twice (the 15 % chance) and came once (Ground Zero 21:01, it looted a container and left a fight at once).
- **The packrat was lost on its way into a group (client).** The setting was 15 %, not 100 %: the server log shows 100 % only on 2026-11-05 until 12:40 UTC, then 15 % at every start (the client sent 15; `com.lir.liveraid.cfg` says 15). But even armed the packrat almost never came: in the armed raid of 2026-11-05 23:48 the kit of "Losyash" (a Glock, no rifle, a big bag) went into a squad of two as a plain PMC (trace: `Losyash=Leader, wizard83=Rifleman`, no `packrat` tag, `kept out of groups 0`). The server tags the profile in the very request that brings it to the client, and the client's `/who` answer was rate-limited to once in 2 seconds, so a tagged profile drawn within 2 s of its arrival was not recognised. Now: a profile with no rifle in an armed raid whose id is unknown makes the pool patch ask the server at once; every planned group asks anew while the packrat is not yet given; and a tagged kit that does get into a group is made the packrat all the same and leaves the group (`LR Packrat: ... got into a group - it is the raid's packrat all the same`; the "leaves its group" code existed but nothing ever called it). Summary: `kit caught in a group and made the packrat N`.
- **Quest steps up high are no longer dropped for "not getting closer".** The step of "Strelba po banochkam" (the Kord on the roof of the bank at (56.6, 32.3, 130.6), the AGS on top of Capital Insight at (101, 34.2, 276.4)): 7 attempts of Questers in 5 raids, 0 completed, each dropped after 2.5-11 minutes at 24-324 m ("no closer than ... after 2.5 min"). The rule compared the STRAIGHT distance, which grows while a bot walks round a building to its stairs and up. It now compares the walking distance (NavMesh path, measured every 5 s; straight line plus twice the height when there is no path).
