# LiveRaid 1.2.6 (test)

Made after an audit of 20 raid traces (2026-11-02..07), the game log, the server log and Player.log.

- **"Online-like spawns" is back on.** In the config of 2026-11-07 the switch stood OFF (its default is on): from the raid of 2026-11-05 13:42 on, 14 raids out of 14 had `0 squads placed on random points` (before: 8-23 per raid). With it off QuestingBots puts every squad on the point furthest from everyone, "Spread squads over the map sides", the minimum distance of 25 m and the player scavs' help at fights do nothing (`0 on the side of a loud fight`). A one-time check at start turns it on again (a marker `0. Internal / Spawn settings revision` keeps a later deliberate "off"); every raid with it off writes a warning to the log.
- **Starting squads apart.** F12 "Starting squads apart, m" (6. Spawns, 120 by default, 0 = as before): at the start of a raid a squad is not put closer than that to the other starting squads; when the free points run out the distance shrinks in steps (`start spread: N squads, nearest neighbour min/median ...` in the raid summary).
- **Reserve PMCs not before minute 5.** F12 "Reserve PMCs not before, min" (6. Spawns, 5; 0 = at once). On Ground Zero the lobby loses people in the first minute (raid 16:56: three dead by 1:21, a reserve squad of three at once, 13 PMCs against a lobby of 10); now nobody joins before the set minute (summary: `reserve held back until minute 5 N times`).
- **The whole map is walked evenly** (F12 "Walk the whole map evenly" + "Pull of fresh ground, %", 3. Map-wide movement, on / 60). Why: over many raids the bots reach 92-96 % of the loot spots, but in ONE raid a PMC squad reached 11-38 % of them and 3-16 of 24 regions of Customs were never entered - a region quest took only 8-20 of its spots (a region of 85 spots: 37 %), and nothing told a bot that the far side was untouched. Now (1) a region is split into compact parts of 12-20 spots (`LR Explore rx_rz#k`) that together cover ALL its spots (open ground only as a sample); simulated on the saved maps: Ground Zero 24 quests, 100 % of the spots in every raid (was 37 %), Customs 41 quests, 70 % - the rest is open ground (was 53 %); (2) `Raid/CoverageLedger.cs` marks a spot visited when any bot stands within 12 m of it on its floor (a spot visited 20+ minutes ago counts as half fresh again); a quest whose spots are untouched weighs up to ~1.7 times more, one just walked ~0.7 (each bot to its own degree, explorers more). Nobody is moved by force. The raid summary and every 5 minutes: `LR Coverage: N of M spots visited by a bot, regions never entered K of L`.
- **No grenade into a wall at your feet.** Four of ~190 kills on Ground Zero were "X killed X" in the first minutes. A grenade is held back when the place it is thrown at is under 9 m away or a wall stands within 4 m of the thrower in that direction (`with a wall or the target too close ... ticks`).
- **Gun condition sliders.** "from" never rises above "to" of a band (the config had band 2 at 95-80); moving one drags the other. (The server already swapped such a pair, so guns were not wrong.)
- **Ammo logistics.** One magazine is not carried to the rig again for 2 minutes and not refilled a third time in 90 s when EFT refuses ("hands controller can't perform this operation": 6 per raid; one 8-round magazine was "taken out of the bag" 7 times) - `repeats of one magazine held back`.
- **Keys.** A key placed into a bot's container was moved twice (EFT logged "Trying to move item to the same place where it is" for 20 of 21 keys); the second move is skipped when the item is already there.
- Not changed after checking: the QuestingBots exception `ChooseNextRandomQuest ... Sequence contains no elements` (7 per raid) happens when a bot has no quest left to pick - probably the scav patrols that refuse objectives outside their area (369 refusals per raid); the bot stays on its patrol. The "dead zones" on Customs (x 325..350, z -75..-25 at height -4) are loot spots with no path on the navmesh (listed in `unreachable/`) - no route points can fix them.
- Version 1.2.6 in the client (both editions), the server and the launcher.
