# LiveRaid 1.0.1

Bots that play like people online, for **SPT 4.1.6**. Needs QuestingBots 1.2.0, SAIN 4.5.1, LootingBots 1.8.0, BigBrain 1.5.0 and Waypoints 1.9.0 (LiveRaid Online: also Project Fika 2.4.x). Install guide: `INSTALL.md`.

## Downloads

- `LiveRaid-1.0.1.zip` - single player (SPT without Fika); it also holds the Online edition, which LiveRaid Launcher switches on
- `LiveRaid-Online-1.0.1.zip` - the same, with the Online edition (Project Fika co-op) already active

## What changed since 1.0.0

- **One GUID for the whole mod, as the Forge requires:** the client plugin, the server mod and the mod page are all `com.lir.liveraid`; the plugin is named `Lir-LiveRaid` (`Lir-LiveRaidOnline`). **Your F12 settings file is now `BepInEx/config/com.lir.liveraid.cfg`** - to keep the old settings, rename `com.liveraid.client.cfg` to that.
- **Taking over a bot is now off by default.** It is raw: after the bot you play dies, the textures of the location may stop loading and sound may break. Switch it on in F12 -> After death at your own risk.
- Everything else is as in 1.0.0: bots travel and loot over the whole map and, with the recording of your route switched on, walk the routes you walk; a full online-style lobby; smart population balance; squad doctrines and tactics; keys and doors; looting, fighting, healing and leaving like a player; English and Russian texts; presets.

# LiveRaid 1.0.1

- **Один GUID на весь мод, как требует хаб:** клиентский плагин, серверный мод и страница мода - `com.lir.liveraid`; плагин называется `Lir-LiveRaid` (`Lir-LiveRaidOnline`). **Файл настроек F12 теперь `BepInEx/config/com.lir.liveraid.cfg`** - чтобы сохранить старые настройки, переименуйте `com.liveraid.client.cfg` в него.
- **Вселение в бота теперь по умолчанию выключено.** Режим сырой: после гибели бота, за которого вы играли, могут перестать грузиться текстуры локации и сломаться звук. Включайте в F12 -> После смерти на свой риск.
- Остальное как в 1.0.0.
